# Africa coverage

Envolio accepts IATA/ICAO flight numbers and airport codes globally. The guided search also recognizes major African airlines and cities across North, West, East, Central, Southern and Indian Ocean markets. Nairobi intentionally asks travelers to choose between NBO and WIL.

Primary schedule, status, OOOI event, position, aircraft and historical data comes from FlightAware. Skylink provides separately matched status, METAR, TAF, NOTAM and exact-tail ADS-B context when available. A secondary status is never merged unless flight identity, route, origin-local date and scheduled time agree. Route-search status matching additionally supports known African IATA/ICAO operator pairs and a unique schedule revision within 20 minutes; multiple candidates remain schedule-only.

Delay calculations compare like-for-like timestamps: scheduled gate-out with actual/estimated gate-out, or scheduled takeoff with actual/estimated takeoff. Once an actual gate or runway event exists, an unchanged estimate cannot replace it. If the corresponding scheduled event is missing, that factor is unavailable rather than on time.

Coverage is still provider- and airport-dependent. A live aircraft position does not establish gate, terminal or OOOI completeness. African flights have no connected flight-specific ATC-slot source; FAA EDCT applies only to eligible US departures. Missing fields are labeled and must not be interpreted as an all-clear.

Representative regression coverage includes Ethiopian Airlines, Kenya Airways, South African Airways, Air Peace, RwandAir and Air Algérie; ADD/NBO, JNB/CPT, LOS/ACC, CAI/CMN and West African city discovery shapes. Before making quantified continent-wide coverage claims, run a longitudinal production matrix by airport, airline and event type under the applicable provider licenses.
